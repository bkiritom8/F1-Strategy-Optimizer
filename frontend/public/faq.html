FAQ Center

Comprehensive answers for strategy users, engineering teams, and competition analysts using DivergeX in high-pressure race workflows.

Product & Workflow

What does DivergeX actually optimize during a race?
DivergeX optimizes timing and compound choice for pit stops, projected finish outcomes, and risk posture across aggressive, balanced, and conservative plans. It combines telemetry, environmental context, and historical priors to propose plans with probabilities rather than single deterministic outputs.
How often should strategy recommendations be refreshed?
Refresh at key moments: safety car deployment, sudden weather changes, pit lane incidents, major tire degradation inflection, and after each pit stop cycle. For stable green-flag phases, refreshing every few laps is sufficient for most use cases.
Can I use DivergeX as a sole decision-maker?
No. Treat DivergeX as decision support. It ranks scenarios and exposes trade-offs; final calls should remain with race engineers and strategy leadership based on current operational constraints and external events.
Why do I sometimes see fallback behavior?
Fallback can activate when model artifacts are unavailable, an endpoint is temporarily degraded, or upstream services are rate-limited. The platform is designed to degrade gracefully and still provide actionable recommendations.

Data, Privacy, and Compliance

What data is stored from my session?
Operationally necessary session data can include selected race context, UI preferences, and non-sensitive usage diagnostics. Sensitive identity and secret tokens are not exposed in frontend storage. Refer to Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy for details.
How do cookies work on this platform?
Essential cookies support session continuity and baseline functionality. Optional cookies are used for analytics and UX improvements. You can accept or reject optional cookies from the consent panel and re-open settings via footer controls.
Can I revoke consent after accepting?
Yes. Open cookie settings from the footer and change your preference. The consent state is updated locally and optional tracking behavior should stop for subsequent events.

UI & Reliability

Why can dropdown text appear unreadable on some Windows/light-mode setups?
Native select rendering differs by browser and OS color management. DivergeX now applies explicit option foreground/background and forced-color compatibility to improve readability in light mode and accessibility modes.
How does DivergeX reduce visual overload risk?
High-contrast red scan effects have been softened, animation intensity reduced, and reduced-motion user preferences are respected to lower visual strain in sensitive environments.
What should I do if a view crashes?
Reload first. If persistent, capture the view, browser, selected route, and a screenshot of the error boundary message. Then report through the contact page with exact reproduction steps.

Quick Ops Checklist

  • Confirm race ID, current lap, and selected driver before interpreting strategy variants.
  • Cross-check aggressive plans against safety car probability and undercut risk.
  • Use balanced variants as baseline when confidence is low or telemetry quality is mixed.
  • Re-run simulations after every major race event or weather shift.
